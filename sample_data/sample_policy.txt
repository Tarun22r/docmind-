Employee Leave Policy — Sample Company Inc.

1. Annual Leave
Full-time employees accrue 18 days of annual leave per calendar year.
Leave accrues monthly at a rate of 1.5 days per completed month of service.
Unused annual leave may be carried over to the following year up to a
maximum of 10 days. Any balance beyond 10 days is forfeited on January 1st.

2. Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical
certificate is required for sick leave lasting more than two consecutive
days. Sick leave does not carry over between years.

3. Parental Leave
Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of paid parental leave.
Secondary caregivers are entitled to 4 weeks of paid parental leave.
Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the child's birth or
adoption date.

4. Requesting Leave
Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 working
days in advance for planned leave. Sick leave should be reported to a
manager as soon as reasonably possible, ideally before the start of the
working day.

5. Public Holidays
The company observes 12 public holidays per year, as published annually
by the HR department. Employees required to work on a public holiday are
entitled to a substitute day off within 30 days.

6. Unpaid Leave
Employees with at least one year of service may request up to 30 days of
unpaid leave per year, subject to manager approval. Unpaid leave requests
must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance.
